# 1. Executive Vision

## Mission
We create handcrafted digital experiences that combine thoughtful strategy, editorial design, modern engineering, and memorable storytelling.

## Vision
CreativeCapy exists to prove that websites can feel as emotionally engaging as they are technically excellent.

## Core Values
- Craft over speed
- Simplicity over noise
- Systems over shortcuts
- Personality over gimmicks
- Long-term quality over trends

- Motion should guide, not entertain.
- Great typography builds trust.
- Design should feel timeless.
